# Lecture 9 Demo — Security Awareness Training

**Time:** ~8 minutes | **Tool:** Claude Code

**Status: In Development**

This demo is currently being built. It will feature an AI-powered security awareness training platform that generates customized phishing simulations, tracks employee completion rates, identifies high-risk departments, and adapts training content based on individual performance.

## What This Will Prove

AI personalizes security training per employee — instead of generic annual videos, each person gets targeted simulations based on their role, access level, and past performance. The result: dramatically higher engagement and measurably lower click rates.

## Planned Features

- AI-generated phishing simulations tailored by department
- Employee risk scoring based on click rates and training completion
- Department-level heatmap showing security culture maturity
- Adaptive difficulty: employees who click get more frequent, harder tests
- ROI calculator: training cost vs breach probability reduction

## CIO Takeaway

> "The human layer is your last line of defense. AI makes training personal, continuous, and measurable — not an annual checkbox exercise."
