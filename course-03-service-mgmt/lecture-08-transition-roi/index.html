90-Day Implementation Roadmap

1

Month 1: Shadow Mode

AI watches and learns. Zero risk. Humans do everything.

  • Week 1-2: Deploy AI, connect to ticket system
  • Week 2-3: AI classifies every ticket silently
  • Week 3-4: Compare AI vs human decisions
  • Target: 90%+ classification accuracy
Risk Level: None
AI never touches a ticket
2

Month 2: Assist Mode

AI suggests, humans approve with one click.

  • Week 5-6: AI drafts responses for top 5 categories
  • Week 6-7: Humans approve/edit with one click
  • Week 7-8: Track approval rate and edit frequency
  • Target: 80%+ approval without edits
Risk Level: Low
Human always in the loop
3

Month 3: Auto Mode

AI resolves top categories. Humans review and handle exceptions.

  • Week 9-10: Enable auto-resolution for top 3 categories
  • Week 10-11: Expand to all categories above 90% accuracy
  • Week 11-12: Full rollout + knowledge base auto-generation
  • Target: 50-70% auto-resolution rate
Risk Level: Managed
Proven accuracy before automation
