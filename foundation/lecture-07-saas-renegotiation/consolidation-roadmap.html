SaaS Consolidation Roadmap

CIO AI Curriculum — Lecture 07: From 25 tools to 16 — phased elimination plan

Executive Summary

This roadmap eliminates 9 redundant or underutilized SaaS tools over 6 months across 4 phases, reducing the portfolio from 25 to 16 tools. Each elimination has a named replacement that's already in the stack — no new purchases required. Combined annual savings: $562,400. Phased rollout minimizes disruption: quick wins first, then progressively larger consolidations with migration support.

Phased Execution Plan

Phase 1: Quick Kills

Month 1 3 tools · $114,000 savings

Zero-disruption eliminations. Tools with < 40% utilization or direct replacements already deployed.

Eliminate
Miro Business
$18,000/yr · 30% utilized · 12 of 40 users
→
Replace With
FigJam (Figma)
Already bundled with Figma Organization license
Migration: Export boards to FigJam. One 30-min training session. Sporadic users won't notice.
+$18,000/yr saved Risk: Very Low Effort: 1 day
Eliminate
Google Workspace
$43,200/yr · 15% utilized · 45 of 300 users
→
Replace With
Microsoft 365 E5
Already licensed for 300 users at 96% utilization
Migration: Move 45 remaining users' Drive files to OneDrive/SharePoint. Redirect Gmail to Outlook. Legacy acquisition artifact — overdue.
+$43,200/yr saved Risk: Low Effort: 2 weeks
Eliminate
Monday.com Enterprise
$62,400/yr · 47% utilized · 28 of 60 users
→
Replace With
Jira Software Premium
Already used by Engineering. Ops team migrates in.
Migration: Export Monday boards to CSV, rebuild as Jira projects. Train Ops team on Jira workflows. Shadow-purchased tool — never IT-approved.
+$62,400/yr saved Risk: Low Effort: 1 week

Phase 2: Communication Stack

Month 2 2 tools · $98,400 savings

Consolidate messaging and video to Microsoft Teams. Higher user impact — requires change management.

Eliminate
Slack Business+
$64,800/yr · 88% utilized · 265 users
→
Replace With
Microsoft Teams
Already included in M365 E5. No additional cost.
Migration: Export Slack history. Set up Teams channels mirroring Slack workspace structure. This will be the most culturally resisted change — Slack users are loyal. Run 30-day parallel period.
+$64,800/yr saved Risk: Medium Effort: 3 weeks
Eliminate
Zoom Business
$33,600/yr · 47% utilized · 142 of 300 users
→
Replace With
Microsoft Teams
Teams meetings + webinars cover all Zoom use cases.
Migration: Update calendar integrations. Migrate recurring meeting links. Only 47% of seats are active — many already use Teams. Easiest consolidation of the two.
+$33,600/yr saved Risk: Low Effort: 1 week

Phase 3: Project Management & Monitoring

Months 3–4 2 tools · $207,600 savings

Consolidate PM tools to Jira. Eliminate Splunk in favor of Datadog. Engineering-led migrations.

Eliminate
Asana Business
$39,600/yr · 44% utilized · 35 of 80 users
→
Replace With
Jira Software Premium
Marketing team joins Eng on Jira. Add Jira Work Mgmt view.
Migration: Export Asana projects via API. Create Jira project templates matching Marketing workflows. Jira Work Management provides a simpler board view for non-technical users. 2 training sessions.
+$39,600/yr saved Risk: Medium Effort: 2 weeks
Eliminate
Splunk Cloud
$168,000/yr · 80% utilized · 12 of 15 users
→
Replace With
Datadog Pro
Add Datadog SIEM module ($~20K). Still saves $148K.
Migration: Migrate Splunk dashboards and alerts to Datadog. Replicate SIEM compliance reports in Datadog Security Monitoring. Eng team already prefers Datadog. Biggest single-tool savings in the roadmap.
+$168,000/yr saved Risk: Medium Effort: 4 weeks

Phase 4: Cloud & Infrastructure

Months 5–6 2 tools · $142,400 savings

Consolidate to single cloud + right-size BI. Longest lead time — infrastructure migrations.

Eliminate
Azure (secondary)
$192,000/yr · 60% utilized · 18 of 30 users
→
Replace With
AWS (primary cloud)
Migrate legacy workloads. Single-cloud strategy.
Migration: Inventory Azure workloads (VMs, databases, storage). Migrate in dependency order. Keep Azure AD for M365 integration only (free tier). Estimated 30% of Azure spend ($58K) moves to AWS; net savings $134K.
+$134,000/yr saved Risk: High Effort: 6 weeks
Eliminate
Tableau Creator
$84,000/yr · 63% utilized · 22 of 35 users
→
Replace With
Power BI (M365)
Already included in M365 E5 license. No cost.
Migration: Convert Tableau workbooks to Power BI reports. Some users already have Power BI via M365. Rebuild data connections to Snowflake/warehouse. Longest learning curve of all migrations — budget 3 training sessions.
+$84,000/yr saved Risk: Medium Effort: 4 weeks
