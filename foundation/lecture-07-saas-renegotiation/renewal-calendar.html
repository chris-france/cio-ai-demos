Pre-Renewal Checklist

90 Days Before

  • Pull utilization data from admin console
  • Identify overlapping tools in same category
  • Research competitor pricing and alternatives
  • Disable auto-renew if possible

60 Days Before

  • Send negotiation email to vendor AE
  • Schedule renewal review call
  • Prepare right-sizing proposal (seats, tier)
  • Get CFO alignment on walk-away number

30 Days Before

  • Finalize negotiation — sign or cancel
  • If canceling: begin migration to alternative
  • Notify impacted users of changes
  • Update SaaS inventory with new terms
