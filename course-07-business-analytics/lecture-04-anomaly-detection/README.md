# Lecture 4 Demo — Anomaly Detection in Business Metrics

**Time:** ~8 minutes | **Tool:** Claude Code

## What This Proves

Business anomalies hide in plain sight until someone runs the weekly report. AI-powered anomaly detection monitors every metric continuously, flags deviations in real-time, and suggests probable causes — turning reactive reporting into proactive alerting.
